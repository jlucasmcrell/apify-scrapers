Choose your MCP tools

One server, four focused profiles. Keep the full catalog or show your assistant only the tools relevant to your work. No separate repository or subscription is needed.

Installation configuration

Requires Python 3.10+, uv (provides uvx), a compatible local MCP client, and your own Apify account. Add this server entry to your client's MCP configuration without replacing other entries.

Replace YOUR_APIFY_API_TOKEN privately in your client, not on this page. This page never asks for or stores your token. Restart the server and reconnect or refresh its tools after changing profiles. Remove the older full-server entry if you want only the focused tools.

The MCP code is MIT-licensed. Actor runs execute in Apify's cloud on your account and are billed at the Store price; other platform charges may apply. Profiles are a tool-selection convenience, not an account permission or spending boundary. Direct Actor API calls are unaffected.

What each profile includes

Business intelligence and lead generation

Google Maps businesses, website technology detection, company registries, contractor licenses, and LEI lookup. Try: find five electricians in Dallas and return their websites and phone numbers.

Jobs, market and consumer intelligence

Job listings, Google News, keyword suggestions, app reviews, vacation rentals, YouTube search, and Twitch streams. Try: find five Google News results about a company, with publisher and article links.

Government and public records

SEC filings and insider transactions, court cases, sanctions, vulnerability records, federal spending, campaign finance, EPA facilities, Census geocoding, grants, EU tenders, and vehicle recalls. Try: retrieve one Microsoft 10-K and its original SEC document link.

Research and health data

Clinical trials, openFDA, CMS healthcare providers, and Europe PMC papers. Try: find five clinical trials about a research topic. Source records are not medical advice.
